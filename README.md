## The policy says 5 business days. The AI answer says 14. Would you release it?

**Policy:** refunds are processed within 5 business days after approval. **AI answer:** refunds are processed within 14 business days. What would you change before releasing the answer?

<details>
<summary>See the suggested answer</summary>

Block the answer. The policy supports 5 business days. The deadline also starts **after approval**. A check that looks for matching words can accept the right number while missing that condition.

</details>
